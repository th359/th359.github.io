Taiga Hayami

I am a researcher at NTT R&D in Japan. I received my M.Eng. from the Graduate School of Fundamental Science and Engineering, Waseda University, in March 2026.

My graduate research focused on implicit neural representations for videos, especially efficient neural video representation and compression. I have studied decoder architectures and methods for improving representation efficiency and reconstruction quality.

Research

I am interested in computer vision, implicit neural representations, neural video representation, and video compression. My academic research explores compact neural representations for videos and high-quality frame reconstruction. I have also worked on frame interpolation and novel view synthesis.

Selected Publications

Selected first-author publications are highlighted below. See Google Scholar for citation information.

Resolution-Flexible Decoding for Hybrid Neural Video Representations
Taiga Hayami, Masaya Takabe, Hiroshi Watanabe
IEEE VCIP 2026 Accepted
arXiv / Conference

A resolution-flexible decoder for hybrid neural video representations using uniform 2× upsampling, spatial alignment, intermediate supervision, and difficulty-aware sampling.
Structure-Preserving Patch Decoding for Efficient Neural Video Representation
Taiga Hayami, Kakeru Koizumi, Hiroshi Watanabe
IEEE MMSP 2025
Paper / arXiv / Code

Structure-preserving patches rearrange video frames into spatially aligned patch images, enabling efficient global-to-local decoding.
SR-NeRV: Improving Embedding Efficiency of Neural Video Representation via Super-Resolution
Taiga Hayami, Kakeru Koizumi, Hiroshi Watanabe
IEEE GCCE 2025
Paper / arXiv / Code

An INR-based video representation framework that incorporates a super-resolution network to recover fine details with compact embeddings.
Neural Video Representation for Redundancy Reduction and Consistency Preservation
Taiga Hayami, Takahiro Shindo, Shunsuke Akamatsu, Hiroshi Watanabe
IEEE ICCE 2025
Paper / arXiv / Code

A neural video representation that separately models high- and low-frequency information to reduce redundancy and improve reconstruction.
Implicit Neural Representation for Videos Based on Residual Connection
Taiga Hayami, Hiroshi Watanabe
IEEE GCCE 2024
Paper / arXiv / Code

A residual-connection approach that uses low-resolution video information to improve reconstruction of fine image details.
